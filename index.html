Questions

What exactly am I getting?

A subscription to the Autostraddle print magazine. Issue #03 — Sporty Spice — ships to you now. After that, every new issue lands on your doorstep as it comes out, four a year, 200+ pages each of essays, photoessays, reported features, fiction, poetry, comics, queer city guides, gay crosswords, astrology and advice. Your subscription also includes full AF+ digital membership — exclusive content on Autostraddle.com, member discounts on everything at For Them, and members-only newsletters.

Which issue arrives first?

Issue #03, the sports issue — it ships when you subscribe. Your next one is Issue #04 when it exists.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, in two clicks from your account. No questions, no hidden fees, no auto-renewal traps.

What if I don't love my first issue?

Email us and we'll refund you. Keep the magazine either way.

Do you ship internationally?

Memberships ship to US addresses only for now. We cover postage on every issue after your first one, and we can't carry that outside the US yet. Single issues are a different story: order one from anywhere and we'll ship it to you.

Who's behind it?

Autostraddle is one of the longest-running independent queer media outlets, published by For Them — both led by queer, trans, and lesbian editors, writers, and photographers.
